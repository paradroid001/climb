# Bird Climb

#### Local multiplayer 2d: 2-(8?) players
- jump and flap your way to beat your opponents to the top.
- collect nuts (eggs?) to grow your flock, grow bigger, and increase your number of flaps.
- throw your family members to knock other birds out of the air.

#### decision:
- do you 'die' if you get forced out of the bottom of the screen?
  - pro: simple, and single screen
  - con: people can be 'out' early, no chance of a comeback.
- or do you just get knocked down until you land on a platform (and lose some flock)
  - pro: always a chance to get back, may be more exciting finishes
  - con: will need split screen

#### Playtest notes
- 0.6 playing with 5 players
  - It was way too chaotic, you kept running into people
	- Make the level wider
	- Zoom the player cameras out a little bit
  - You would get stuck at the bottom platforms with not enough flock to do much:
	- There weren't enough powerup spawns
	- They should probably periodically respawn
	- Flock which have been launched may not be turning back into powerups
    - There should be other ways to get up (teleporter, spring)
	- There should be more ways for the leaders to come undone (moving platforms, spikes)
  - Shooting felt like there wasn't much ROI
	- Shots should pulled toward targets roughly in the direction they are moving, ideally the leader.
	- Shots should neutralise other shots, i.e. shots from different owners should become powerups when they collide
